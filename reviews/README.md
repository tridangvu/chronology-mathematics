# Article quality reviews

Completed validation sheets live here.

1. Read [`docs/quality-reference.md`](../docs/quality-reference.md) (authoritative quality standard, v1.0).
2. Copy [`docs/quality-validation-sheet.md`](../docs/quality-validation-sheet.md) to `reviews/<article-id>--<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`.
3. Fill every criterion with a localised justification. Do not mark `Satisfait` without a concrete control.
4. Keep the sheet with the Git commit (or other unambiguous version id) of the article examined.

**Publication gate:** an article may move to `status: published` only when a review decides **prêt à publier** with no open blocking defects (see the quality reference). Agents and authors must not publish on style alone or on an incomplete evaluation.
