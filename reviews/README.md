# Article quality reviews

Completed validation sheets live here.

1. Read [`docs/quality-reference.md`](../docs/quality-reference.md) (authoritative quality standard, **v1.1**).
2. Copy [`docs/quality-validation-sheet.md`](../docs/quality-validation-sheet.md) to `reviews/<article-id>--<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`.
3. Fill every criterion with a localised justification. Do not mark `Satisfait` without a concrete control. **C4 (Example)** and **C5 (Comments)** may be **Non applicable** when no corresponding content exists.
4. Keep the sheet with the Git commit (or other unambiguous version id) of the article examined.

**Publication gate:** an article may move to `status: published` only when a review under **v1.1** decides **prêt à publier** with no open blocking defects (see the quality reference). Agents and authors must not publish on style alone or on an incomplete evaluation.

**Archive:** sheets written under older criteria (e.g. v1.0) belong in [`archive/`](./archive/) and are **not** validations under v1.1.
